📖 How to Use This App

Follow these 7 simple steps — you'll be tracking in 2 minutes.

1
🎯Set Your Goal First
→ Tap the Goal tab at the top.
→ Choose Male / Female, then enter your age, weight (kg), and height (cm).
→ Pick your Activity Level and weight goal (maintain / lose / gain).
→ Press “Calculate & Save Goal”. Your daily kcal target appears.

2
🍎Add the Food You Eat
→ Go to the Track tab.
→ In Search Food, type a name (e.g. “hummus” or “chicken”).
→ Pick the food from the list, then enter the weight in grams — or use a Quick button (50g, 100g…).
→ Check the preview, then press “+ Add to This Day”.

💡 Tip: Sodium and Cholesterol fill bars update live — watch them turn amber at 75% and red when over the daily limit.
3
🏃Track Exercise with the Timer
→ Tap the Move tab.
→ Scroll the activity list and tap ▶ on the exercise you're doing (e.g. “Walking 5.6 km/h”).
→ The timer starts automatically. The selected row glows green.
→ When done, press “Reset & Log”. Calories burned are added to the day.

💡 Tip: Each exercise row fills with colour showing how much of your daily target it contributed. Green → amber → red → shimmering gold at 100%.
4
✍️Add Manual Burn (Optional)
→ On the Move tab, find the dashed box “Add manual active kcal”.
→ Type kcal burned (e.g. 80 for walking to work).
→ Press Add — it goes straight into the total burn.
→ Made a mistake? Press Reset to clear today's manual entries.

💡 Note: The Reset button only clears the manual entries — it does not affect timer-logged exercises.
5
🌈Understand the Colours
→ Every progress bar uses the same colour language:
Green — safe zone (0–75%)
Amber — approaching limit (75–90%)
Red — over the goal (90%+)
Gold — exercise bonus! (100%+)
Example: exercise target 200 kcal, you did 250 kcal
+50 kcal bonus 🎉
💡 Sound: You'll hear a rising chime at 100%, 125%, 150% and 200% of your exercise target.
6
📅Switch Between Days
→ On the Track tab, top area: use ‹ and › to go one day back or forward.
→ Or tap the date field to pick any day from a calendar.
→ Or scroll to the bottom and tap any day in “All Days”.

7
💾Save & Restore Your Data
→ Your data is auto-saved in this browser every time you add something.
→ For a portable backup, tap 💾 Save As HTML — it downloads a full copy of the app with your data inside.
→ To restore on another device, open that saved HTML file or press 📂 Restore and pick a JSON backup.
